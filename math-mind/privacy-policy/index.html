Overview

Math Mind is an English-language math and logic puzzle game intended for teenagers and adults ages 13 and older. This policy explains the information stored on your device, how Android backup and device transfer may handle that information, and how Google advertising and consent services process data in the production app.

Information stored on your device

Math Mind stores the following information in the app's private local storage:

  • Your game progress, represented by the highest unlocked level.
  • Your appearance preference: System, Light, or Dark.
  • Whether sound effects are enabled.
  • Whether background music is enabled.
  • Whether haptic feedback is enabled.

These values are stored with Android Preferences DataStore. Math Mind does not require an account. Answers entered during a puzzle, the currently displayed screen, and temporary gameplay feedback are not saved as persistent profile data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for Math Mind. Depending on your device and Android settings, Android may copy Math Mind's progress and preference files to an Android-managed cloud backup or transfer them to another device.

The backup or transfer service is provided and controlled by Android, your device manufacturer, or your chosen backup provider. KookyPhantom Games does not operate a server that receives these backups and cannot access or restore them for you. You can manage backup and device-transfer options through your device and account settings. Backup retention and deletion are governed by your backup provider.

Advertising in Math Mind

Math Mind uses Google AdMob in the production app. A rewarded ad may be offered when you ask to reveal a puzzle hint. When a rewarded ad is available, the hint is revealed only after the ad reports that the reward was earned. If ads cannot be requested because consent is not available, the hint remains usable without requiring an ad.

Math Mind may also show an interstitial ad after a first-time progress milestone, currently after every fifth completed level that advances your saved progress. These interstitials appear only at the transition after level completion. Replaying an already completed milestone does not trigger another milestone interstitial, and an unavailable or failed ad does not block progression.

Math Mind does not use banner ads or app-open ads.

Google advertising and consent services

Math Mind uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage applicable consent and privacy messages. Depending on your region and applicable requirements, Math Mind may display a European regulations message, a US state privacy message, or another message configured through Google UMP.

Math Mind requests updated consent information when the app launches. Ads are initialized and requested only when Google UMP reports that ads may be requested. When Google UMP requires an in-app privacy-options entry point, a Privacy choices action appears in Settings so that you can reopen the privacy form and review or change available choices.

Data handled by Google Mobile Ads

According to Google's disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK may automatically collect and share the following categories of data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • App interactions or product-interaction data, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video interactions.
  • Diagnostic information about the app and SDK, such as launch time, hangs, and energy usage.
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other available device or account-related identifiers.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security. The exact data used can depend on your device, region, consent choices, Google settings, ad-serving mode, and whether an identifier is available.

For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, and Google UMP privacy guidance.

Your privacy choices

Where required, Google UMP presents consent or privacy choices before Math Mind requests ads. If Privacy choices is available in Settings, you can use it to reopen Google's privacy form. You can also manage or delete your Android advertising ID through your device's privacy and advertising settings where supported.

If consent information cannot be updated and Google UMP does not permit ad requests based on an existing choice, Math Mind keeps ads disabled for that session. Gameplay progression remains available, and hints remain usable.

Other services and information

Math Mind does not require an account and does not include a separate analytics service, crash-reporting service, authentication service, or social-networking service. KookyPhantom Games does not operate an app server that receives your saved progress or preferences. Google advertising and consent services process data as described above.

Support email

If you voluntarily email gamesupport.kp@gmail.com, KookyPhantom Games will receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is received through the email service rather than collected by Math Mind and is used to understand and respond to your support request. Do not include information that is not needed for support.

Retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until they are changed through the app, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Copies held by an Android-managed backup or device-transfer service may remain according to that provider's settings and retention rules.

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads and UMP is handled under Google's policies and the privacy choices and controls available to you. You can revisit available consent choices from Math Mind's Settings when Google UMP requires that option, and you can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through supported device settings.

Children's privacy

Math Mind is intended for users ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The advertising and consent practices described above apply to the production app and remain subject to applicable regional requirements and the choices presented through Google UMP.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Math Mind's features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published.
