Overview

Math Mind is an English-language math and logic puzzle game intended for teenagers and adults ages 13 and older. This policy explains the limited information that Math Mind stores on your device and how Android backup and device transfer may handle that information.

Information stored on your device

Math Mind stores the following information in the app's private local storage:

  • Your game progress, represented by the highest unlocked level.
  • Your appearance preference: System, Light, or Dark.
  • Whether sound effects are enabled.
  • Whether background music is enabled.
  • Whether haptic feedback is enabled.

These values are stored with Android Preferences DataStore. Math Mind does not require an account. Answers entered during a puzzle, the currently displayed screen, and temporary gameplay feedback are not saved as persistent profile data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for Math Mind. Depending on your device and Android settings, Android may copy Math Mind's progress and preference files to an Android-managed cloud backup or transfer them to another device.

The backup or transfer service is provided and controlled by Android, your device manufacturer, or your chosen backup provider. KookyPhantom Games does not operate a server that receives these backups and cannot access or restore them for you. You can manage backup and device-transfer options through your device and account settings. Backup retention and deletion are governed by your backup provider.

Information Math Mind does not collect

Math Mind does not request Internet access and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, or social-networking services. The app does not collect or transmit your name, email address, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, puzzle activity, progress, or preferences to KookyPhantom Games or another app-operated service.

Support email

If you voluntarily email gamesupport.kp@gmail.com, KookyPhantom Games will receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is received through the email service rather than collected by Math Mind and is used to understand and respond to your support request. Do not include information that is not needed for support.

Retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until they are changed through the app, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Copies held by an Android-managed backup or device-transfer service may remain according to that provider's settings and retention rules.

Children's privacy

Math Mind is intended for users ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Math Mind's features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published.
